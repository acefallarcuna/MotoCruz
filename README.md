# MotoCruz

A comprehensive web-based inventory management system designed specifically for motoshops, featuring an integrated analytics dashboard to provide real-time insights into business operations.

MotoCruz was built to modernize and streamline the management of a motoshop. It provides owners and managers with powerful tools to track stock, monitor sales, and make data-driven decisions to optimize their business. The intuitive dashboard transforms raw inventory data into actionable insights, helping to boost efficiency and profitability.

### Features

* **Real-time Inventory Tracking:** Easily add, update, and track motorcycle parts, accessories, and other products with real-time stock levels.
* **Integrated Analytics Dashboard:** A powerful dashboard that visualizes key business metrics, including sales trends, popular products, and stock turnover rates.
* **Supplier and Order Management:** Manage supplier information and track incoming orders to ensure a smooth supply chain.
* **Sales and Transaction History:** Record and review sales transactions, providing a complete history for financial reconciliation and business analysis.
* **User-friendly Interface:** A clean and intuitive design that makes it easy for motoshop staff to manage inventory and access data.
